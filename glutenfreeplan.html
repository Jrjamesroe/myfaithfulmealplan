Breakfast

No Bake Granola Bars
  • 1 tsp coconut oil or avocado oil
  • 1.5 cup pitted dates
  • 1/4 cup nut/seed butter
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup roasted unsalted almonds, roughly chopped
  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1/4 cup pumpkin seeds
  • 1 Tbsp ground flax seeds

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Line an 8" square pan with parchment paper and leave a slight overhang on all sides.
  2. Very lightly grease your parchment paper with coconut or avocado oil just to ensure the bars don't stick.
  3. Chop your dates. You can either do this by hand or in a food processor. The dates will form a ball in the food processor, and that's okay!
  4. Transfer your dates to a mixing bowl.
  5. In a small saucepan over medium/low heat, melt the nut/seed butter of choice with the honey and vanilla. Stirring occasionally until combined and warm. This should take about 1 minute.
  6. Add the mixture to the bowl with the dates and mix to combine.
  7. In a small saucepan over medium/low heat, melt the nut/seed butter of choice with the honey and vanilla. Stirring occasionally until combined and warm. This should take about 1 minute.
  8. Add the mixture to the bowl with the dates and mix to combine.
  9. Fold in the almonds, oats, pumpkin seeds, and flax.
  10. Press the mixture into the prepared pan and freeze for about 30 minutes. Remove your parchment paper with the frozen bar mixture onto a cutting board and cut into 12 bars. Store in the refrigerator.

Snack

Gluten-Free Breakfast Casserole
  • 1 lb ground pork breakfast sausage
  • 1 large shallot
  • 6 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon pepper
  • 8 oz sour cream
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 8 oz shredded sharp cheddar cheese
  • 4 cups frozen shredded hash browns

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees then spray a 9×13" baking dish with nonstick spray and set it aside.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat then add the breakfast sausage and shallot or onion and cook through. Drain if necessary then return the sausage to the pan and set aside to cool slightly (can be done ahead of time).
  3. To a very large mixing bowl, add the eggs, salt, and pepper then whisk to combine. Add the sour cream and milk then whisk to combine. Add 3/4 of the shredded cheese, the cooked and slightly cooled breakfast sausage, and thawed hash browns then stir to combine.
  4. Pour the mixture into the prepared baking dish then spread into an even layer. Sprinkle the remaining cheese on top then cover the dish with a piece of nonstick-sprayed foil and bake for 60-75 minutes or until the center has set and a knife inserted into the center comes out clean. Remove the foil then bake for 5 more minutes or until the cheese has turned golden brown.
  5. Let the casserole sit for 15-20 minutes before slicing and serving.

Lunch

Chicken Greek Pasta Salad
  • 1 lbs chicken breast
  • 1 1/2 tbsp Greek seasoning
  • 8 oz Gluten Free pasta, cooked according to package directions (Use your favorite shape)
  • 1/3 cup black olives, chopped
  • 1/4 cup banana peppers, chopped
  • 13 oz garbanzo beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1/3 cup green olives, sliced
  • 1/4 cup fresh dill, chopped
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil, chopped
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 3 tbsp red wine vinegar

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Preheat oven to 400-450 degrees.
  2. Brush both sides of your chicken breast with olive oil and season it with half of the Greek seasoning and half of the salt.
  3. Place the chicken in the oven for 20 mins.
  4. Add your olive oil, vinegar, and remaining salt and Greek seasoning to a small bowl and whisk to combine.
  5. Chop your chicken into bite sized pieces and add to a serving bowl.
  6. Add the remaining ingredients to the bowl Top with the Greek dressing and toss to combine.

Dinner

Slow-Cooker Moroccan Chicken
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil or avocado oil
  • 2-3 pounds boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 onion thinly sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic minced
  • 3 tablespoons almond butter
  • 3/4 cup chicken stock
  • 1 teaspoon brown sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon ginger
  • 1/2 teaspoon coriander
  • 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
  • pinch of cayenne pepper
  • 1 pound baby carrots
  • 1/2 cup dried apricots
  • 1/4 cup dried cherries

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Season the chicken on both sides with salt and pepper. In a large skillet, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil over medium-high heat. When hot, add 1/2 the chicken thighs and cook on each side for 3-4 minutes. The chicken doesn't need to be cooked through, just browned on the outside for flavor. Transfer the chicken to the slow-cooker then repeat with the remaining chicken thighs.
  2. Over medium heat, add the sliced onions to the now-empty skillet. Cook, scraping up the brown bits off the bottom of the pan until softened, about 3-4 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the chicken stock and scrape the bottom of the pan. Whisk in the almond butter and salt, sugar and spices.
  4. Add the onion/stock mixture to the slow-cooker. Stir to combine then place the carrots on top. Cook for 4 hours on low.
  5. In a small saucepan over medium/low heat, melt the nut/seed butter of choice with the honey and vanilla. Stirring occasionally until combined and warm. This should take about 1 minute.
  6. After 4 hours, add the apricots and cherries and cook for another 1-2 hours.
  7. Shred the meat slightly and serve over cooked quinoa. Enjoy!
